```
Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

```

```
NUVEXA ("the App") is a personal growth, habit-tracking, activity, challenge, and progress-management application.

This Privacy Policy explains how NUVEXA handles information when you use the App.

1. Information Stored by NUVEXA

NUVEXA allows you to create and manage information within the App, including:

  • Username and nickname
  • Profile picture
  • Activities and habits
  • Challenge information
  • Activity and challenge completion status
  • Progress information
  • Notification and reminder settings
  • Other preferences configured within the App

This information is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage/database functionality.

NUVEXA does not currently require you to create an account or provide an email address, telephone number, or password.

2. Profile Pictures

If you choose to add a profile picture, NUVEXA uses the device's image-selection functionality to allow you to select an image from your device.

The selected profile picture is stored locally on your device. NUVEXA does not upload your profile picture to a NUVEXA server.

3. Notifications

NUVEXA can provide local notifications for features such as daily reminders and challenge reminders.

Notification schedules and related preferences are stored locally on your device.

NUVEXA's notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. The App does not use a remote push-notification server to send these reminders.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings and the notification settings available within the App.

4. Data Sharing

NUVEXA does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

NUVEXA does not currently provide user information to advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

5. Data Transmission

Based on the current functionality of the App, NUVEXA does not transmit the personal information you enter into the App to a NUVEXA-operated cloud service or remote database.

Your locally stored profile, activity, challenge, and progress information remains on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

NUVEXA uses third-party software libraries to provide certain functionality, such as local database storage, image selection, and local notifications.

These libraries are used to provide functionality within the App. NUVEXA does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about your use of the App.

7. Data Security

Because your NUVEXA information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

We recommend using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock, device encryption, and keeping your operating system up to date.

8. Data Deletion

Because NUVEXA stores its application data locally, you can remove locally stored NUVEXA data by using the data-management or deletion functionality provided by the App, where available, or by uninstalling the App from your device.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored application data depending on your device and operating-system behavior.

If NUVEXA introduces accounts, cloud storage, or other remotely stored data in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the applicable data deletion process.

9. Children's Privacy

NUVEXA is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a remote service.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NUVEXA's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
```
